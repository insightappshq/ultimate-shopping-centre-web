## Our approach in one paragraph

USC uses only what it needs to work: **essential cookies** that keep you signed in and remember your delivery location, and a few **browser storage** entries for your own convenience. We do **not** use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking or third-party analytics cookies, and we do not sell or share cookie data with advertisers.

## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. They let the site recognise your browser on later visits — for example, to keep you signed in. Similar technologies, such as your browser's local storage, store small pieces of information on your device but are not sent to our servers automatically.

## Cookies we use

| Name | Purpose | Type | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `sb-<project>-auth-token` (may be split into `.0`, `.1`, …) | Keeps you securely signed in. Holds your authentication session issued by our authentication provider, Supabase. | Essential | Up to 400 days, or until you sign out |
| `sb-<project>-auth-token-code-verifier` | Protects certain sign-in and email-verification flows against tampering. Only set when needed. | Essential | Deleted once the sign-in step completes |
| `usc_location` | Remembers your delivery location (area name, pincode and approximate coordinates) so prices and delivery times match where you shop. | Essential | 1 year |

Essential cookies are required for USC to function. Because they are strictly necessary to provide the service you request, they do not require your consent, but we still tell you about them here.

## Browser storage we use

| Key | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `theme` | Remembers whether you prefer light, dark or system theme. | Until you clear browser data |
| `usc:recent-searches` | Shows your recent searches as suggestions in the search box on this device. | Until you clear browser data |
| `usc:cookie-notice` | Remembers that you've seen our cookie notice so we don't show it again. | Until you clear browser data |

This information stays on your device and is not used to identify or track you.

## Third parties

When you open a product on a shopping platform (for example Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart, BigBasket, JioMart, DMart Ready or Amazon), you leave USC. That platform may set its own cookies, governed by its own cookie and privacy policies. USC does not control those cookies.

Our service providers — such as our authentication, hosting and email-delivery providers — process data on our behalf to operate USC. They do not set advertising cookies on our site.

## Managing cookies

You can view, block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Most browsers also let you clear local storage for a specific site. Please note:

- Blocking or deleting the authentication cookie will sign you out, and you won't be able to use USC while it is blocked.
- Deleting `usc_location` resets your delivery location; you'll be asked to confirm it again.

Helpful guides: [Google Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647), [Safari](https://support.apple.com/guide/safari/manage-cookies-sfri11471/mac), [Mozilla Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/kb/clear-cookies-and-site-data-firefox) and [Microsoft Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/microsoft-edge/delete-cookies-in-microsoft-edge-63947406-40ac-c3b8-57b9-2a946a29ae09).
